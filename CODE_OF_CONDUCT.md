# Code of conduct

Crane is a small project, and we want it to be a good place to take part in.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults or discrimination, in any channel the project uses.
- Help newcomers; everyone started somewhere.

If someone breaks this, or you feel unsafe, tell the maintainer privately: open a [private report](https://github.com/jhtjernsmo/crane/security/advisories/new) on this repository (only maintainers can see it) and say it is about conduct. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, issues or contributions that break these rules, and block people who keep doing it.

This is based on the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
